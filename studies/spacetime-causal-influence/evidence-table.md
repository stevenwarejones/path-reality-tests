# Primary evidence and closest prior work

Coverage date: 2026-09-26 UTC. This is an annotated, bounded comparison, not a
systematic review or priority certificate. “Full” means relevant methods and
assumptions in the indicated version were read; supplements are identified
separately. No source data or paper PDFs are redistributed here.
