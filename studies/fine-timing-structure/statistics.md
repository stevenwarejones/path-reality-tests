# Estimands and simultaneous coverage

## Current interventions over realized histories

For receiver/local setting y, pretrial history H, selected-event indicator D,
and retained ordered pair T=(pulse bit, decoded phase), define

    F_xy(t) = (1/N) sum_i P(D_i=1,T_i<=t | H_(i-1),do(X_i=x,Y_i=y)),
    Delta_y(t) = F_1y(t)-F_0y(t),  K_y = sup_t |Delta_y(t)|.

The notation <= denotes each frozen grid cut (phase strictly below the next
integer boundary in that pulse). Strict versus weak comparison at an arbitrary
point does not change the supremum, but the implementation's cuts are explicit.
These are full-trial event subdistributions; missing selected events contribute
to a separate no-event atom. No conditioning on detection, no remote outcome,
no coincidence matching. The response law is averaged over realized histories,
not over histories generated by sustained interventions on earlier settings.

Assume per-history exogeneity and joint assignment propensity
q_xy,i in [.25-epsilon,.25+epsilon]. Given these premises, each binary feature
C_i=1{X_i=x,Y_i=y,D_i=1,T_i in A} has predictable mean
mu_i=q_xy,i p_xy,i(A). Past outcomes, device memory and changing response laws
may be arbitrary. Correct recorded trial correspondence and the meaning of the
current setting must hold. Missing physical trials outside the archive are not
covered by the following count-completion argument.

## Finite-family inversion

For fixed positive lambda, E[exp(lambda C_i)|H] <=
exp(mu_i (exp(lambda)-1)). Products give a nonnegative supermartingale. The
same argument applies to -lambda. For M labels, 10 fixed positive lambdas and
both signs, use b=log(2*10*M/.005). A union bound (or Ville's inequality) gives
simultaneously, at the fixed endpoint,

    L(k)=max(0,max_lambda (lambda*k-b)/expm1(lambda)),
    U(u)=min(N,min_lambda (-lambda*u-b)/expm1(-lambda)).

Trusted count k and compatible uncertain-event budget v give the pathwise
completion k <= sum C_i <= u=k+v. No predictable-missingness assumption is
needed for this enclosure. Propensity inversion gives p(A) in
[L/(N(.25+epsilon)), U/(N(.25-epsilon))] intersect [0,1]. The feature contrast
interval is [l_1-u_0,u_1-l_0]. Intersections that exclude every compatible law
are flagged and not silently converted to a discovery.

A pays M=3888 labels: two receivers, four joint contexts, 486 thresholds.
B pays M=6512: four contexts times (event-incidence feature plus all event bins
at all five resolutions), summed over both receivers. Alice event-bin counts
are 3,9,69,243,486; Bob 3,9,69,249,486. Both use alpha=.005, for a joint .01.
Duplicate/overlapping features are paid for conservatively, not assumed independent.
No-event is derived by normalization; it needs no extra Bernoulli feature.

All epsilon values are nested sensitivity assumptions on the same count event.
The two completion envelopes bound the same counts if their respective premises
hold; no minimum-p selection occurs. The .01 budget is for this extension only,
not a guarantee for all analyses already tried on this archive. The protocol
was frozen after the earlier study was seen. This is a conditional retrospective
analysis, not a claim that researcher selection was itself randomized.

## CDF bands and unresolved cells

Intersect arm bands with monotonicity: lower at cut j is the maximum lower
bound over cuts <=j; upper is the minimum upper over cuts >=j. The band for
Delta then yields

    L_Kgrid=max_j distance(0,[l_j,u_j]),
    U_Kgrid=max_j max(|l_j|,|u_j|).

For an interior point of cell j, monotonicity alone gives
F_x(left_j) <= F_x(t) <= F_x(right_j). Hence |Delta(t)| is at most
max(U_1(right_j)-L_0(left_j), U_0(right_j)-L_1(left_j)); left of the first
cell both CDFs are zero. The maximum of these bridges and U_Kgrid bounds
K_full. The grid lower bound also lower-bounds K_full. This uses no unproved
IID Kolmogorov-Smirnov calibration and no assumption of uniform within-cell mass.
An incidence difference can cause a CDF difference without a timing-shape change.

## Additive tree, normalization, TV and information loss

All partitions refine the original no-event/early/core/late mapping. Event bins
form a tree rooted at event incidence. For each arm, intersect every parent's
interval with the sum of its children's intervals, bottom-up. Then tighten
child i using [L_parent-sum_(j!=i) U_j, U_parent-sum_(j!=i) L_j], top-down.
These operations give the feasible interval projections of the additive tree;
independent linear programs check selected projections. They are not a sharp
optimization over uncertainty assignments, the two arms, or the TV functional.
Unknown rows can enter multiple feature upper bounds but must not be interpreted
as having actually completed in all those bins at once. Tree normalization
removes some such inconsistency; the remaining region is an outer enclosure.

Let event mass a_x=sum_event p_x, d_c=p_1c-p_0c. No-event contrast is a_0-a_1.
For each bin contrast interval, let near_c be its distance to zero and far_c
its maximum absolute endpoint. With all outcomes including no-event,

    L_TV=max(max_c near_c, .5 sum_c near_c),
    U_TV=min(.5 sum_c far_c, max(U_a0,U_a1), 1).

The event cap follows from TV <= .5*(|a_1-a_0|+a_1+a_0)=max(a_0,a_1).
It is useful in this sparse archive: hundreds of low-count bins otherwise
accumulate large empty-cell penalties. Enforce true TV monotonicity over the
nested levels by propagating lower bounds upward in resolution and upper bounds
downward. These bounds need not tighten when resolution increases.

For each level J=TV_level-TV_coarse>=0, report
[max(0,L_level-U_coarse), min(1,U_level-L_coarse)]. Coarse J is identically
zero. A positive lower bound establishes dependence lost under this particular
coarsening; it does not identify a causal mechanism. TV_full for the retained
pair has lower bound L_width1 and upper bound max(U_a0,U_a1). This full-law cap
leaves arbitrary sub-cell redistributions unresolved.

## Limits of interpretation

The event-supported budget assumes all relevant detector records survive in
order; clock excursions and ambiguous labels still carry no trusted phase
restriction. Under unrestricted completion, even a row without a recorded
detector can contain an event. Known setting labels restrict compatible arms;
ambiguous ones can be either. Unknown intervals from either receiver and their
existing guards are retained exactly. We do not infer q bounds from frequencies,
certify spacelike ordering, or transport an original Bell calibration to these
new estimands. No rejection proves unusual physics; no non-rejection proves
agreement of recorded laws. Integer raw tags do not identify unrestricted analog
sub-bin delays, regardless of the sophistication of a digital test.
