## Search for a complementary calibrated family

1. **Alternate Clifford RB deposit — downloaded and reconstructed.** [Extra manifest](extra-manifest.json) pins the 3,078,406-byte file, SHA-256 `d86a4d1907b0ef80d09b69bb9c6f3b6ff11825873924e4ee533eb49236e0448d`. All 2,004 ordered count pairs and every primitive expansion match the existing RB records. The 48 Clifford definitions are parsed as literal data, never executed. This removes a convention ambiguity but adds **zero independent observations**; it cannot anchor readout or improve statistical precision.
2. **Other experimental dates in the same deposit — metadata only.** [Pinned tree inventory](results/deposit-inventory.json) records Git blob IDs and sizes for the 2014, February 2015 and other March 2015 files. Their filenames do not certify common gates with March 30. These files were not treated as calibrated interventions or shared-parameter data. No claim about their uninspected contents is made.
3. **Operational quantum-memory experiment — main article and public supplement inspected.** Yuan et al. [npj Quantum Information (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-021-00444-9), [author-hosted primary PDF](https://bartoszregula.me/pdf/s41534-021-00444-9.pdf), and its publisher supplement (pinned in [literature manifest](literature-manifest.json)) provide a serious alternative family pairing: noise-dependent memory games and sequential two-qubit operations. The experiment uses IBM `ibmq-ourense`, the first three qubits, and reports 8,192 shots per input/output setting. This is not the ion apparatus. Supplement VII specifies ancilla constructions and six alternating-control CX sequences. Tables I–III distinguish experimental `IBMQ`, simulated `QASM`, and theoretical columns. They contain setting probabilities rounded to four decimals and scores, **not a separate per-acquisition SPAM calibration likelihood or timestamped joint count archive**. For example, `0.2937` corresponds uniquely to `2406/8192` if ordinary nearest rounding and that fixed denominator are assumed. That conditional reconstruction does not validate every postprocessed marginal, the same-acquisition calibration, or the trusted-input assumptions. The damping construction explicitly combines marginals conditioned on an ancilla; it is not a raw single binary circuit count. The public supplement was checked, not merely its availability statement. Defer a full-class joint resource analysis until setting-level count semantics and a matched preparation/readout calibration likelihood are established. This is a precise bridge not verified here, not a claim that no such data exist. No authors were contacted.
4. **Earlier audited alternatives — no invented bridge.** The earlier study downloaded Quantinuum SPAM/RB gadget records and superconducting leakage-control data, and documented absent physical-ion/schedule or calibration-to-job links. Those audits are preserved. A calibrated leakage gadget would constrain a detector/transfer ambiguity, but its operation is not the unknown binary ion detector. No leakage bound from that hardware enters this study. Nairobi's two epochs also remain separate motivation, with no certified shared gate set.

The smallest useful additional product for the **displayed scale orbit** is a binomial count record for a trusted Z measurement of the same reset, together with its independent calibration and acquisition match. In the enlarged model it constrains `(1+s*c)/2`; the two physical points differ by more than 0.0044564. A future 2–4-family analysis must keep the calibration's uncertainty and nuisance assumptions in one confidence construction and delete it as a source in its own right. An ideal target label or fitted GST state is not that record.

The new composition lemma is elementary qubit geometry combined with a shared suffix. We have not established that the inequality itself is novel. The measurable increment is a reproducible full-class, uncertainty-aware **one-sided** prediction and physical countermodel for a previously unrecorded word. Its margin is substantial, but the unproven GST-only direction prevents the requested two-source conclusion. The EB example rigorously delimits uniqueness; its tiny crossing and standard gauge mechanism do not establish a major advance. No companion formalization PR is warranted for the elementary algebra alone.
