# Retrospective discovery history

The initial [protocol](protocol.json) was written after inspecting PR15/PR17 and current main but before the new target screen. It froze the model class, unchanged confidence construction, complete-source deletion rule, three target routes, controls and stopping criteria. It did not preregister a target or reserve validation data.

1. Reconstructed all 6,661 rows from the six hash-pinned files. Inventoried the original deposit at its immutable commit. Confirmed the additional Clifford RB representation duplicates the existing measurement rows rather than constituting a third source.
2. Screened every one of the 2,004 RB rows through five transformations in order: `ww`, `w GxGx`, `GxGx w`, `w GyGy`, `GyGy w`. Excluded expanded words appearing anywhere in either complete source. Duplicate proposed targets remain as separate screen entries. Surviving counts were 1,995, 1,994, 1,994, 1,994 and 1,998, totaling **9,975**. The complete ordered family hash is `b64478be9ceb4b7959b507d3f259f417642d62132dafee30003ca82dabcbce17`. The source-wide generation rule and hash replace a bulky list; full-source verification reconstructs it.
3. Evaluated the three prior physical witnesses on every candidate. Analytic repeat/append bounds were screened; prepend candidates had no nonvacuous inequality in this method. There were 410 append entries with lower bound exceeding the RB-only point by more than `1e-6`. Selected the largest such difference, then shortest word, then hash. This outcome-dependent rule selected `GiGiGyGy`, not an independently held-out target. [Screen results](results/screen.json) contain all family summaries and primary data.
4. Searched the **complete GST region** for a crossing physical witness, minimizing this target with all 4,657 GST cells and six GST groups. SLSQP used 49 free coordinates, fixing only redundant process-factor scales at indices 0,16,32. All shared SPAM coordinates remained free. Bounds are exactly `sqd_general.bounds()`, shifted inward by `1e-10`, with finite-difference step `1e-4`, `ftol=1e-9`, 80-iteration cap and desired constraint margin `1e-6`. Initial point was the prior joint-compatible model; this is a numerical starting point, not a claim of GST-only-trained prediction. Seed 0 used that point; seed 260927 added independent normal perturbations of standard deviation 0.015 to the 48 process coordinates before clipping. Best feasible points had targets 0.77141414 and 0.77155931. Both runs hit the iteration limit. Histories, parameters and all probabilities are saved. An initial JSON serialization failure was repaired before the saved runs; it was not treated as a scientific outcome. No optimum or impossibility claim follows.
5. Aggregate control: exact commuting pi flips required an effect eigenvalue above one and were rejected. A common-sign angle perturbation did not yield a feasible effect in the explored range. Opposite-sign perturbations `Rx(pi+delta), Rx(pi-delta)` were then screened on a 3,001-point grid from 0 to 0.03. At each grid point two linear equations matched family means, with `0<=l<=u<=1` required. The selected saved grid point was `delta=0.005189999999999999`. Saved factors and counts define the final certificate; [aggregate reconstruction](aggregate_discovery.py) independently reproduces the recipe. This control intentionally drops all cells and ten length groups, retaining just two overall means.
6. Resource discovery: examined scalar Bloch similarities near the prior joint point and repeated-Gy entanglement-breaking transitions. The final rational scale 1009/1000 and block length 10,749 were chosen retrospectively to straddle a PPT boundary while keeping native channels and SPAM physical. This was boundary-seeking, not a preselected robust resource task. Only the saved final scales/block/vector are certified; no exhaustive scale or length optimum is asserted. A complete trial log of the exploratory scale/length inspection is not retained; the final choice and its certificate are reproducible. Exact telescoping proves all-word equality; interval calculations prove physicality and opposite classifications.
7. Final verification does not use optimizer success flags or finite-bank spread. It proves the analytic bound over the full declared physical class, checks every retained count/group at each saved point, independently binds probabilities to original words, and checks the resource task with a separate length-appropriate interval calculation. Prospective binomial testing and context sensitivity are separate hypothetical calculations.

Stopping point: an explicit RB-only crossing witness, joint feasible point and aggregate-only control are certified. A complete GST-only crossing witness is missing. The resource orbit proves a particular failure of unique identification but not a failure of useful joint interval tightening. The most direct next mathematical step is a stronger compositional outer bound for a target with credible witnesses on **both** deletion sides. For the resource route, a matched independently calibrated SPAM/reference record is needed to break the demonstrated orbit; the public IBM supplement documents tasks and rounded probabilities but no verified matched calibration likelihood.
