The count audit must not be described as overturning the tomography paper.
It diagnoses a conditional shared-acquisition model at finite precision and
identifies the original circuit/preprocessing records needed to settle its
interpretation. The prospective theorem provides a transparent bridge from
complete reset calibration to a full-class bound, but no measured archive has
been validated for that bridge here.

The follow-up [register-aware model](flagged-instrument.md) makes the classical
register and feed-forward explicit, following the same operational distinction.
Its EB-reference/diamond-distance inequality is an elementary extension, not
a claimed novel theorem. The new increment is measured source reconstruction:
IQ classification and hardware-unit matching, complete selected terminal QPT
counts, NMN alternative permutations, and safe White job-schema inspection.
The [decision ledger](acquisition-semantics.md) marks empirical quantities
unevaluated; [missing-acquisition.md](missing-acquisition.md) identifies exactly
which additional records would make the established sufficient test usable.
No complete empirical identifiability result or exhaustive novelty audit is
claimed.
