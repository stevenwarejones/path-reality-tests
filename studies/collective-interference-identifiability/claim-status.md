## Concrete next gate

First obtain from already released data a defensible bound on
`2 * sum(transfer_TV) + event_error` below 0.00032675, or strengthen the
statistical certificate enough to tolerate a justified larger error. In
particular, independently calibrate the other input sites; copied shifted
images do not do this. A new measurement is only a proposed remedy, not an
authorized acquisition. It may be preferable to switch to an archive with
interleaved per-input calibrations and all accepted/rejected records.

If pursuing full-source separation, define one simultaneous region for all
retained count constraints and exhibit a physically valid source-only
countermodel for the complete higher-order family as well as calibration.
A weaker relaxed bound after removal is not that witness. Establish full
quantum feasibility on that same region before claiming a required resource
rather than an inconsistent apparatus description.
