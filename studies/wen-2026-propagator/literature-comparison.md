# Primary-literature comparison for the Wen 2026 study

Source review and refresh performed on 2026-09-26 UTC. The verified deposit,
all-sheet mapping and descriptive Figure 3/4 replication are now available.
This table separates prior literature from the conditional numerical comparisons
in [method-comparison.md](method-comparison.md).

| Source | Material reviewed | Role and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| [Feynman (1948)](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.20.367) | Primary abstract and bibliographic record | Historical equivalence of descriptions; the finite matrix/path identity is independently derived in the companion Lean case-study documentation. This was not a complete historical or relativistic survey. |
| [Wen et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41566-023-01212-1) | Author manuscript measurement derivation and paraxial mapping | Experimental lineage of propagator inference; retain preparation, reference and pointer calibration assumptions. |
| [Wen et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeh1011) | Main text/XML; supplement Sections 5–10 and captions; rendered formula page; Dryad metadata/README | Audit target. Distinguish pointer signals, inferred propagators, multiplied histories and the independent endpoint acquisition. Source review alone does not establish available trial covariance. |
| [Lundeen et al. (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10120) | Author manuscript introduction, measurement derivation and experimental description | Predecessor for interpreting direct optical reconstruction as ensemble inference with normalization/reference assumptions. |
| [Matzkin (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.2.032048) | Author manuscript v2 main derivation and discussion | Weak probes can be described through selected amplitudes and a postselection denominator. Supplemental derivations have not been comprehensively audited here. |
| [Magaña-Loaiza et al. (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms13987) | Primary full-text theory, results and methods | Aperture changes can change boundary conditions; any comparison of differently filtered propagation needs its own physical correspondence. Spatial loops do not establish backward-time motion. |
| [Pusey (2014)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.1535v2) | Primary abstract and theorem scope; refreshed bibliographic/version record | A contextuality route requires experimentally verified conditions beyond an anomalous-looking weak value. Not applied to Wen's deposited tables here. |
| [Kunjwal, Lostaglio and Pusey (2019)](https://arxiv.org/html/1812.06940v2) | Sections II.3–II.6, III.2 and IV in the earlier primary-text review; scope/version refreshed | Finite-pointer, noise-robust formulations require operational equivalences and disturbance information. The proposed extension is not a theorem already certified for this apparatus. |

The new phase-intervention protocol and its stronger contextuality extension are
separate proposed work. Their design, literature gates and open questions belong
to the phase-intervention study, not to a finding about Wen's data.

The current source/version/code refresh is recorded in the search log. No usable
additional author implementation or correction was identified, with explicit
search/access limitations. The real column mappings and missing repeat-level
records are documented in the data dictionary. A stronger ontological claim
requires additional operational assumptions and an exact specification of the
alternative being excluded.
