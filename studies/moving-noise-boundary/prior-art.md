# Claim-to-prior-art audit

This is a bounded audit, not a novelty claim. The mathematical ingredients are
standard covariance, Wick/Dyson, operator norm and window inequalities. No Lean
companion is warranted. The new repository work is their explicit, conservative
application to the archived response bridge and its failed complementarity gate.

| Topic | Primary work | Established before this study | Delta and limitation here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colored and dissipative interference | [Toroš, Gasbarri, Bassi, 1601.03672](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.03672) | Comparing collapse-modified interference with macroscopic localization; colored-response assumptions | Norm bound avoids using a short-memory optical map, but cannot validate the SSB witness |
| Colored mechanical constraints | [Carlesso, Ferialdi, Bassi, 1805.10100](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.10100) | Frequency-dependent force response and colored-noise bounds | Exact sampled-line kernels and conservative projection bound; no new empirical exclusion |
| Frame dependence | [Adler, 1807.11450v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.11450v3) | Colored noise and frame dependence | One explicit Earth-following law; not an all-inertial-frame certificate |
| Noise filtering under arbitrary control | [Green et al., 1211.1163v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.1163v2) | Spectral control responses and higher-order correlated-noise expansions | Conservative mass/variance/exposure norm bound; no claim to invent filter functions or Wick expansion |
| Cold-atom collapse bounds | [Bilardello et al., 1605.01891](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.01891) | Cold-atom expansion/heating, including colored/dissipative models | Public CAL images acquired and decoded; relative-force kernel tested; calibrated lens/imaging/motion bridge not established |
| LPF collapse bounds | [Helou et al., PRD 95, 084054](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.95.084054) | LPF used to constrain collapse noise | No new LPF exclusion; missing calibration/motion join remains |
| Rotation/translation complementarity | [Altamura et al., 2501.08971](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08971) | Geometry-based torque/force comparison, gas-background caveats | No novelty from adding torque; no subtraction without a background premise |
| Calibrated spacecraft response | [Armano et al., 1806.08581](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08581), [2405.05207](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05207) | Dynamical calibration and mission-wide noise/filter analysis | Distinguish housekeeping, predicted orbit and calibrated science products |
| Spatial spectral optimization | [Piccione, 2511.00644v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00644v2) | Optimization of spatial smearing | Spatial Gaussian fixed here; arbitrary smearing not covered |
| Positive-measure optimization | [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/) | Linear optimization over nonnegative objects and dual upper certificates | No optimized dual or global maximum is claimed; positivity only proves the restricted-band bounds |

Searches included cold-atom public source data/PSI, LPF PreProcessed/calibrated
science, Gaussian noise/Wick/Dyson norm bounds, filter-transfer functions, and
colored collapse motion. Broad searches returned unrelated results; absence of a
relevant hit is not evidence of novelty. Primary abstracts and archive metadata
were opened; the CAL full text and existing repository's detailed paper audit
were read. No comprehensive new full-text literature review is claimed.
